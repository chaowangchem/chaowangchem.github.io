Ethylene-forming enzyme (EFE) is a non-heme iron (II)- and 2-oxoglutarate-(Fe(II)/2OG)-dependent oxygenase that exhibits distinct catalytic activity. While most Fe(II)/2OG-dependent oxygenases catalyze the substrate hydroxylation accompanied by the decarboxylation of the 2OG cosubstrate to succinate, EFE primarily converts the 2OG into CO<sub>2</sub> and ethylene.
